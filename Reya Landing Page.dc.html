Pervolia’s name is rooted in the Greek for orchard. Fields of wheat and artichoke guide your eye along the horizon, punctuated by the mountains’ silhouette and the calming presence of the Mediterranean sea. Reya marks a new chapter in the village story: modern, well-built villas conceived around light, air, space, and the pleasure of being outdoors.

J+A Architects, Design Partners

106 villas. One address.

Reya embraces the warm, inclusive Cypriot essence: large enough to form a real neighbourhood while staying familiar, holding hands with the village centre.

Every villa sits on its own individual plot with a separate title deed.

An address with a real heartbeat.

A coastal promenade runs along the shoreline, connecting the village to the peninsula. A morning walk along the water before the heat builds. A cycle out to the lighthouse and back before work. An evening meal where the air carries salt and the pace is slow by design. These are not weekend activities; they are the texture of ordinary life here. Pervolia does not perform. It simply exists, and it does so well.

Cyprus Permanent Residency

One investment. Two outcomes.

The qualifying threshold for Cyprus Permanent Residency is €300,000 net of VAT on a new-build property purchased from a developer. The entry price at Reya is set at this level by design. Cyprus is an EU member state with a 60-day annual residency requirement, a favourable personal tax regime, and an international airport 20 minutes from Perivolia.
